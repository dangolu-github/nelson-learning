Passive Voice Foundations

Find the sentence jobs first. Then move the receiver and build the passive verb.

1. Three sentence jobs

StarterWho or what begins the action.
VerbThe action in the sentence.
ReceiverWho or what gets the action.

Active: My grandfather built this house in 1981.

Passive: This house was built by my grandfather in 1981.

3. When passive voice helps

  • Use active voice when the starter and action are the focus.
  • Use passive voice when what happened to the receiver is more important.
  • Use passive voice when the doer is unknown or unimportant.
  • Add by + doer only when the doer gives useful information.

4. Build the passive

  1. Find the starter.
  2. Find the verb.
  3. Find the receiver.
  4. Move the receiver to the front.
  5. Choose the correct form of be.
  6. Use the past participle.
  7. Add by + doer only if it matters.
