Choose the Job, Then Choose the Words

Use the J-O-B decision routine for a model-text gap.

Class goal
Identify what the missing language must do, choose a form that fits, and read the completed sentence aloud.
J — JobIs it an adverb, noun phrase or verb phrase?
O — OptionsWhich choices fit both the meaning and the sentence?
B — BuildComplete it, read it aloud and check the connection.

1. Model Text: Label the Job Before You Match

For gaps 1–7, first write the word or phrase job: adverb, noun phrase or verb phrase. Then match options a–g.

The chart compares monthly visits to three community learning centres from January to June.

There were two broad patterns: one centre became less popular, while the other two attracted more visitors. Attendance at North Hub fell 1 ________ from about 82,000 visits in January to 46,000 in June.

South Point experienced 4 ________ from January to March, but after that attendance 5 ________, ending at approximately 92,000. The overall trend for River Space was also upward. Its visitor total 6 ________ between January and April and 7 ________ to around 74,000 in June.

a significantly
b a gradual increase
c also ended the period higher
d a slight fall
e then climbed sharply
f doubled
g rose steadily
