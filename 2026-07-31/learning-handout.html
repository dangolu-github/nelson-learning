Passive Voice Review

Choose the form that matches the sentence. Then rewrite an active sentence so that the receiver of the action becomes the subject.

1. The quick route

Find the receiver.
Who or what receives the action?
Keep the time.
Past, present, or future?
Build the passive.
be + past participle

Time matters: when I arrived needs a past form; since 1985 needs a present-perfect form; next week needs a future form.

2. Practice A — Choose the correct option

1. Shakespeare wrote / was written lots of famous plays.
2. When I arrived at the garage, my car was repairing / was being repaired.
3. This painting has sold / has been sold six times since 1985.
4. Your car will repair / will be repaired next week.
5. My book won’t finish / won’t be finished until next year.

3. Practice B — Rewrite in the passive voice

1. Thousands of people will visit the museum next month.
2. We will sign the contract as soon as we receive the first payment.
3. The president will answer any questions after his speech.

4. Check before you finish

  • Did I move the receiver to the start?
  • Did I choose the correct form of be for the time?
  • Did I use the past participle?
  • Did I keep the useful time phrase and any useful doer?
