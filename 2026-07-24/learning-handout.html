2. Useful Skill: A-P-C

Key Ideas

  • Understand the visual before choosing graph language.
  • Select two or three main patterns instead of listing every figure.
  • Say the relationship clearly before writing it.
A — AxesWhat do the labels, units, dates and scale mean?
P — PatternsWhich lines rise, fall, cross, peak or finish together?
C — ComparisonsWhich two facts show the strongest contrast or similarity?
Then writeUse your selected patterns, not every number.

4. Notice, Say, and Build

OVERVIEW Overall, Tea Room income fell, while the other three cafes finished above their starting points. Wi-fi Cafe showed the largest increase and ended with the highest income.

DETAIL Tea Room income fell significantly from almost $160,000 to about $50,000 by December.

What to notice

Underline the two main patterns selected for the overview.
Circle the subject and the trend verb in the detail sentence.
Box the manner phrase and the value/time phrase.

Speaking Model

MAIN PATTERN Tea Room income fell overall.

IMPORTANT CHANGE After several fluctuations, it dropped sharply near the end of the year and finished at about $50,000.

COMPARISON By contrast, Wi-fi Cafe ended with the highest income.

Notice how each sentence adds one new piece of meaning. Do not read this model during your own answer.
