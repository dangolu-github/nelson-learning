What Is Narration?

Creative writing can tell a story, describe a moment, or combine both. We began with the language used for telling a story.

Class goal
Explain the difference between narration, a narrative, and a narrator.

Three connected words

Narration

The act, process, or method of telling a story.

Narrative

The story or account that is told.

Narrator

The voice or person that tells the story.

Example: In a story about a difficult journey, the journey and its events form the narrative. The voice telling those events is the narrator. The way that voice presents the events is the narration.

Narration gives the reader a route

A reader needs to understand who is involved, where the events happen, what happens, and how one event leads to the next. Clear narration makes that route easy to follow.
